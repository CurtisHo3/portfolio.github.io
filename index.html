About Me

Hello, I'm Curtis Ho.

Through this portfolio, I aim to showcase my skills, experiences, and projects that highlight my professional drive in Mechatronics, Robotics, and Environmental Engineering. I aspire to build efficient solutions that address global challenges. My creations are guided by the pursuit of elegant design and sustainable functionality, often drawing inspiration from natural elements. While some of my prior climate-related project work remains confidential due to non-disclosure agreements, the projects included here demonstrate my technical rigor and analytical problem-solving. I excel in team settings through challenging conceptual boundaries, prompting peers to question design assumptions. Looking ahead, I envision a career path focused on sustainable infrastructure, particularly technologies that reduce emissions and improve transportation, such as magnetic levitation systems and hydrogen as a scalable energy storage option. I am also intrigued by how robotics and automation unlock the potential of AI to revolutionize energy and transit infrastructure.
